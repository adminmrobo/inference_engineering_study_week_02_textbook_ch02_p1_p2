# zh (简体中文)
- Simplified Chinese. Use full-width punctuation （，。：；？！“”） in Chinese sentences; keep half-width inside code, math, numbers and HTML.
- No spaces between Chinese characters; one space between Chinese and Latin words is optional — be consistent (no space preferred except around English terms of 2+ words).
- 2,350억 → 2350亿, 220억 → 220亿. Keep digits exactly otherwise.
- Characters: 博士, 助手, 果蝇研究员, Money-Robo. Doctor's verdict short: “……。果蝇胜。”
